# Coach check-in — 2026-11-18

Seven weeks. That's how long it's been since this plan was built (Oct 1)
and the clock started on a 10-week intensive. `PROGRESS_LOG.md` has never
had a single row filled in — not a baseline score, not one drill, not one
mock. Zero logged minutes against a plan that assumes 90–120 min/day,
6 days/week.

This is not a "fall behind and catch up" problem. A 10-week plan with
7 weeks of silence isn't 70% behind — it's not started. Catching up on the
original schedule is off the table. Continuing to add new drill content on
top of an unused plan would just be more shelf-ware. So: the plan restarts,
smaller and harder to dodge.

## What actually happened (best guess, correct me)

Nothing was illegible or missing — the materials were sitting there ready
to use. The likely failure mode is the one that kills most self-study
plans: the first task was too big. "Take a full timed mock of your weakest
section" is a reasonable Week 0 task and also exactly the kind of task
that's easy to keep postponing, because it has no natural 20-minute
version.

## The reset

Forget Week 0–7 of `STUDY_PLAN.md`. As of today, this is Week 1, Day 1,
and the only job is to break the zero.

**Today, before anything else, in one sitting:**

1. Open `IELTS/writing_task2.md`. Pick one prompt. Set a real timer for
   40 minutes. Write the essay. Stop at 40:00 whether you're done or not —
   that's the actual exam constraint, not a suggestion.
2. Self-grade it against the rubric in that file.
3. Open `PROGRESS_LOG.md` and fill in exactly one row in the Baseline
   table: date, "IELTS Writing T2", your self-graded band, time taken,
   one honest sentence on what went wrong.

That is the entire assignment. Not three exams. Not a full mock. One
essay, one timer, one logged row. If that doesn't happen today either,
the problem isn't the plan.

## Non-negotiable going forward

- No new drill content gets added to this repo until `PROGRESS_LOG.md`
  has at least 5 logged rows. More material is not the bottleneck.
- `STUDY_PLAN.md`'s Week 0 baseline requirement stands, but it's now
  satisfied incrementally — one section at a time, not "a full timed
  mock of each exam" in one go. Baseline rows accumulate in
  `PROGRESS_LOG.md` as you go; there's no gate that requires them all at

- Targets are unchanged (IELTS 7.5+, TOEFL 105+, GRE V162/Q165+). Nothing
  about the difficulty of this got easier — only the size of the first
