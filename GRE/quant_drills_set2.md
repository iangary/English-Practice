# GRE Quant — Drill Set 2 (harder tier, tighter clock)

**Timer: 1:30/question, not 1:45 — 12 questions in 18 minutes flat, no
exceptions.** Set 1 gave you the standard pace; this set removes 15
seconds/question because the real exam doesn't slow down for you once
you hit a harder passage. If you blow the clock on this set, that's the
data point — don't quietly re-time yourself slower and call it done.

No calculator beyond the on-screen four-function one. Read every problem
twice. These are written to contain at least one trap each (an "except,"
an inclusive/exclusive boundary, an integer constraint, or a units
switch) — missing the trap, not the arithmetic, is what costs people
points at this level.

## Quantitative Comparison

**1.** x and y are integers. x > 0, y < 0, and x + y = 3.

Quantity A: x
Quantity B: |y|

**2.** Set S consists of the integers from 1 to 50, inclusive, except for
all multiples of 7.

Quantity A: The number of integers in S
Quantity B: 43

**3.** A rectangular solid has integer edge lengths and a volume of 60.

Quantity A: The greatest possible surface area of the solid
Quantity B: The least possible surface area of the solid, multiplied by 3

## Problem Solving

**4.** A tank is filled by Pipe A alone in 6 hours and by Pipe B alone in
10 hours. Both pipes are opened together, but Pipe B is shut off after 2
hours. How many additional hours does Pipe A alone need to finish filling
the tank?

**5.** In a group of 120 people, 70 speak French, 65 speak German, and 25
speak neither language. How many people speak both French and German?

**6.** A sequence is defined by a₁ = 3 and aₙ = 2aₙ₋₁ − 1 for n > 1. What
is a₅?

**7.** The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 5:4. After 6 more girls
join and no boys leave, the ratio becomes 5:6. How many boys are in the
class?

**8.** A circle is inscribed in a square of side length 8. What is the
area of the region inside the square but outside the circle, in terms of
π?

**9.** If p and q are prime numbers greater than 2, which of the
following must be even? Indicate all that apply.
(A) p + q
(B) p · q
(C) p² + q²
(D) p + q + 1

**10.** A car travels from City A to City B at 60 mph and returns along
the same route at 40 mph. What is the car's average speed for the entire
round trip, in mph?

**11.** Data interpretation (no chart given — compute from stated figures):
In 2019 a company's revenue was $4.2 million, 30% of which came from
Product X. In 2020, total revenue grew by 25%, and revenue from Product X
grew by 50%. What percent of 2020's total revenue came from Product X?

**12.** x is a positive integer such that x³ has exactly 4 digits.
Quantity A: The number of possible values of x
Quantity B: 4

## Answer key (do not peek before finishing all 12)

1. Since y = 3 − x and y < 0, x must be greater than 3. For any such x,
   |y| = x − 3, which is always less than x. Quantity A (x) is always
   greater. Answer: (A).
2. Multiples of 7 from 1–50: 7,14,21,28,35,42,49 = 7 numbers. 50-7=43.
   Answer: (C), equal.
3. Factor triples of 60 (l×w×h): (1,1,60) SA=2(60+60+1)=242;
   (3,4,5) SA=2(12+20+15)=94; most "cube-like" factoring minimizes SA.
   Greatest SA uses most elongated triple (1,1,60)=242. Least SA uses
   (3,4,5)=94. 94×3=282 > 242, so Quantity B is greater: (B).
4. Pipe A rate 1/6/hr, Pipe B rate 1/10/hr. Together 2 hrs: 2(1/6+1/10) =
   2(8/30)=16/30=8/15 of tank done. Remaining 7/15 of tank, Pipe A alone
   at 1/6/hr: time = (7/15)/(1/6) = 42/15 = 2.8 hours.
5. Speak at least one language: 120-25=95. French+German-Both=95.
   70+65-Both=95 → Both=40.
6. a1=3, a2=2(3)-1=5, a3=2(5)-1=9, a4=2(9)-1=17, a5=2(17)-1=33.
7. Let boys=5k, girls=4k. (5k)/(4k+6)=5/6 → 30k=20k+30 → 10k=30 → k=3.
   Boys=15.
8. Square area 64, circle radius 4, circle area 16π. Region = 64−16π.
9. p,q odd primes >2, so both odd. (A) odd+odd=even ✓. (B) odd×odd=odd ✗.
   (C) odd²+odd²=even ✓. (D) even+1=odd ✗. Answer: A and C.
10. Average speed = 2·(60·40)/(60+40) = 4800/100 = 48 mph (harmonic mean,
    NOT 50 — this is the classic trap).
11. 2019 Product X = 0.30×4.2M = 1.26M. 2020 total = 4.2M×1.25=5.25M.
    2020 Product X = 1.26M×1.5=1.89M. Percent = 1.89/5.25 = 36%.
12. x³ has 4 digits: 1000 ≤ x³ ≤ 9999. Cube root of 1000=10, cube root
    of 9999≈21.5. So x ∈ {10,11,...,21} = 12 values. Quantity A=12,
    Quantity B=4. Answer: (A).

## Scoring

11-12 correct in 18:00 or under → you're at the level this set targets.
8-10 correct → log which trap category (inclusive/exclusive, "must be"
vs "could be," rate problems, units) cost you the most, and re-drill
that category specifically before moving on.
≤7 correct, or over time → do not advance to full-length Quant sections
yet. Go back to Set 1 and rebuild at the standard pace first.
