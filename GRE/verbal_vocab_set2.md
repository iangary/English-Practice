# GRE Verbal — High-Frequency Vocabulary, Set 2 (30 words)

**Drill protocol:** Same as Set 1 — word→meaning, then meaning→word, both
directions under 3 seconds, twice in a row on different days before you
mark it "known" in `PROGRESS_LOG.md`. Set 2 only starts once Set 1 is
fully mastered, not "mostly familiar." Half-known words are the ones that
cost you the point on test day, not the ones you've never seen.

## Drill: fill in the blank (cover the answer key below)

1. The new evidence did not just clear his name — it ______ his original,
   widely mocked theory.
2. Her ______ praise of every proposal, no matter how weak, made her
   feedback worthless to the team.
3. After weeks of denial, the executive finally ______ and admitted the
   numbers had been inflated.
4. The therapist noted a ______ agreement between the couple to avoid the
   one topic that actually mattered.
5. Despite losing the election, she gave a ______ concession speech,
   praising her rival's campaign without reservation.

### Answer key
1. vindicated
2. obsequious
3. capitulated
4. tacit
5. magnanimous
