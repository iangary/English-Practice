## Drill: word pairs — same direction or contrast?

GRE Text Completion blanks are driven by logical signal words, not vocab
alone. For each pair below, identify whether the two words/concepts work
**with** each other (same-direction) or **against** each other (contrast):

1. Obsequious / Sycophant
2. Temerity / Captious
3. Mendacious / Gainsay
4. Perfunctory / Zenith
5. Disabuse / Mendacious

### Answer key
1. Same-direction (an obsequious person often behaves like a sycophant)
2. Unrelated enough to need context — do not force a relationship that
   isn't there; this is itself the trap GRE likes to set
3. Contrast-adjacent (gainsaying implies disputing a claim, which is
   plausible for mendacious — i.e., dishonest — testimony)
4. Contrast (perfunctory = minimal effort; zenith = peak achievement)
5. Same-direction (disabusing someone of a mendacious belief is the
   natural pairing)

## Timed combined drill (Set 1 + Set 2, 60 words)

Give yourself exactly 8 minutes to go word → meaning across all 60 words
from both sets, out loud or written. Log your miss count in
`PROGRESS_LOG.md` under a new row: `GRE Vocab Combined`. Fewer than 3
misses in 8 minutes = ready to move on to timed Text Completion sets that
mix both vocab tiers. 3+ misses = repeat this combined drill daily until
you clear it; do not add new words on top of an unstable base.
