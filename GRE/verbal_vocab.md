# GRE Verbal — High-Frequency Vocabulary, Set 1 (30 words)

**Drill protocol:** Go word → meaning first (cover the right side). Then
go meaning → word (cover the left side, read only the definition, produce
the word). Only mark a word "known" in `PROGRESS_LOG.md` once you can do
both directions in under 3 seconds each, twice in a row, on different days.

## Drill: fill in the blank (cover the answer key below)

1. The CEO's ______ explanation seemed to answer the question while
   actually avoiding it entirely.
2. After years of research, the ______ field of quantum computing is
   finally attracting serious investment.
3. Her ______ one-word answers made the interview feel like pulling teeth.
4. The new regulations aim to ______ the environmental damage caused by
   the factory.
5. Despite the criticism, he remained stubbornly ______, refusing to
   change the proposal at all.

### Answer key
1. obfuscating / specious (context supports either — "obfuscating" fits
   best if emphasizing deliberate unclarity)
2. nascent
3. laconic
4. mitigate
5. recalcitrant

## Why this matters for Text Completion specifically

GRE Text Completion rarely fails you on "do you know this word" in
isolation — it fails you on whether you correctly identified the
sentence's **logical direction** (same-direction clue vs. contrast clue)
before you even got to picking the word. Drill the logic in
`text_completion_practice.md` using these words once you've learned them.
