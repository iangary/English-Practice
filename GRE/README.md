# GRE General Test

## Format cheat sheet (current GRE format, ~1h58m total)

| Section | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Analytical Writing (Issue task) | 30 min | 1 essay, analyze an issue |
| Verbal Reasoning | 2 sections, ~41 min total | Text Completion, Sentence Equivalence, Reading Comprehension |
| Quantitative Reasoning | 2 sections, ~47 min total | Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Data Analysis |

Scored 130–170 per section (1-point increments) for Verbal/Quant, 0–6
(half-point increments) for AWA.

## Where people plateau

- **Verbal:** vocabulary is necessary but not sufficient — most missed
  Text Completion/Sentence Equivalence questions come from misreading the
  **logical signal** (contrast vs. support) in the sentence, not from not
  knowing a word.
- **Quant:** the GRE is not "hard math" — it's heavily a test of careful
  reading and not falling for deliberately laid traps (e.g., "x > 0" vs.
  "x is an integer > 0"). Most errors at the 165+ level are careless, not
  conceptual.
- **AWA:** a 6.0 essay doesn't require more sophisticated ideas than a
  4.0 one — it requires that every claim is explicitly supported and that
  counterarguments are anticipated, not just a stronger thesis statement.
