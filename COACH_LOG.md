# Coach Log

This is where I, as your coach, check in on this repo and tell you the
truth about where you stand — not where you'd like to stand. Entries are
blunt on purpose. If you want encouragement without accountability, this
is the wrong training camp.

distinguished from "I've been looking at the files." Those are not the
