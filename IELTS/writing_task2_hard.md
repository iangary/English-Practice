# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Hard Tier (Band 8+ prompts, compressed time)

**Unlock condition:** You should already be self-grading 7.0+ on
`writing_task2.md` prompts before moving here. If you're still below 7.0,
more volume at this difficulty trains bad habits under pressure instead of
fixing the actual gap — go back and fix Task Response/Cohesion/Lexical
Resource/Grammar one at a time on the base set first.

**Rules:** 32 minutes, hard stop (8 minutes tighter than the real 40, to
build a time buffer for test day). Same 250-word minimum. No notes
prepared in advance — these prompts are intentionally two-barreled or
abstract, which is where Band 6–7 writers start padding and Band 8+
writers stay precise.

## Self-grading addendum for this tier

At Band 7+, graders stop asking "is this correct?" and start asking "is
this precise, or just safe?" Add these checks on top of the base rubric:

- **Specificity test:** Underline every noun phrase that could apply to
  literally any essay on any topic ("many problems," "various factors,"
  "a lot of people"). More than 2 in a 250-word essay caps you at Band 7
  regardless of grammar — Band 8+ requires concrete, topic-specific
  examples, not placeholder abstractions.
- **Counterargument test:** For opinion essays, does your essay
  acknowledge the strongest version of the opposing view before refuting
  it, or only a weak strawman? Band 8+ raters notice the difference.
- **Sentence-variety test:** Count your sentence openings. If more than
  half start with the subject ("This is...", "People believe...",
  "Governments should..."), your syntax is flatter than Band 8 requires.

## Prompts (two-barreled and abstract — the hard-tier signature)

**Opinion, with a built-in tension:**
1. Some argue that success is mostly the result of hard work, while
   others believe it is largely determined by factors outside a person's
   control, such as luck or family background. To what extent do you
   agree that success is within an individual's control?
2. It is sometimes said that a country's cultural identity is weakened
   when it becomes more economically developed and globally connected.
   To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**Discuss both views, abstract framing:**
3. Some people believe that the primary purpose of punishment for a
   crime should be to rehabilitate the offender, while others believe it
   should be to deter future crime and protect society. Discuss both
   views and give your own opinion.
4. Some think that the increasing use of artificial intelligence in the
   workplace will ultimately benefit society by freeing people from
   repetitive labor; others believe it will cause more harm than good by
   eliminating livelihoods faster than new ones can be created. Discuss
   both views and give your own opinion.

**Problem/solution, systemic (no single obvious fix):**
5. In many countries, young people are increasingly disengaged from
   traditional politics, such as voting in elections. What do you think
   are the causes of this, and what measures could be taken to address
   it?
6. As more people rely on digital devices for information, some argue
   that critical thinking and the ability to evaluate sources are
   declining. What problems does this cause, and what can be done to
   address them?

**Two-part question, forcing a position on tradeoffs:**
7. Some people believe that scientific research should be funded
   primarily by governments, while others believe private companies
   should fund it. Which do you think is more effective, and why?
8. In many developed countries, the population is aging rapidly. Is this
   a positive or negative development for society as a whole?

## After you write: the Band 8+ audit

- Read only your topic sentences, in order, with nothing else. Do they
  form a coherent summary of your argument on their own? If you need the
  supporting sentences to understand your own structure, a Band 8+ reader
  will too, and they won't have time to dig for it.
- For every example you used, ask: "Could this sentence appear unchanged
  in an essay about a completely different topic?" If yes, it's not
  specific enough — replace it with something that only makes sense for
  this exact prompt.
- Time how long your conclusion took to write. If it's under 90 seconds,
  it's probably a restatement, not a synthesis — Band 8+ conclusions add
  a qualifying thought (a condition, a caveat, a forward-looking
  implication), not just a summary.

Log the band estimate, the single biggest flaw, and whether you beat the
32-minute cap in `PROGRESS_LOG.md`. If you're consistently over 32 minutes
here, that's your real bottleneck — fix pacing before vocabulary.
