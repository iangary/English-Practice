# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Drill Set 2 (Band 8–9 tier, tighter clock)

**Rules: 35 minutes, not 40.** Five minutes off the standard clock, same
250-word minimum. These prompts are also deliberately more abstract than
Set 1's — less "agree/disagree with a concrete policy," more "untangle a
genuinely two-sided abstraction under time pressure." That combination
(less time, harder prompt) is intentional: Band 7 writers handle one of
those two constraints; Band 8–9 writers handle both at once.

## Self-grading addendum (on top of Set 1's rubric)

At this tier, also score yourself on:

5. **Idea sophistication** — Band 7 answers state the obvious position
   and support it. Band 8+ answers acknowledge the strongest version of
   the counter-argument before dismantling or qualifying it. If your
   essay never mentions why a reasonable person might disagree with you,
   cap yourself at Band 7 on this axis regardless of how clean the prose is.
6. **Precision over hedging** — Count instances of "some people think,"
   "it could be argued," "in some ways." More than two per essay signals
   you're hedging instead of committing to a specific, defensible claim.

## Prompts

**Abstract opinion:**
1. Some argue that the concept of "work-life balance" is a modern
   invention that previous generations did not need and should not be
   treated as a right. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
2. It has been said that a society's progress should be measured by how
   it treats its most vulnerable members, not by its average standard of
   living. To what extent do you agree?

**Discuss both views, abstract:**
3. Some believe that true creativity requires total freedom from
   constraints, while others argue that constraints are what force
   genuine creativity to emerge. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
4. Some contend that forgetting is as cognitively important as
   remembering, and that a society obsessed with permanent digital
   records is losing something valuable. Discuss both views and give your
   own opinion.

**Two-part, higher abstraction:**
5. As automation replaces more cognitive (not just manual) labor, some
   argue humans will be freed to pursue more meaningful work, while
   others argue most people will simply become economically redundant.
   Which view is more convincing, and why?
6. Does a nation's culture shape its economic outcomes, or do economic
   conditions shape a nation's culture? Discuss both directions of
   influence and give your own view.

## Post-writing audit (do this in addition to Set 1's checklist)

- Find the sentence where you address the counter-argument. If you can't
  find one, your essay is Band 7-capped on Task Response no matter how
  fluent the English — go back and add one, timed separately (5 min), to
  see what it would have taken.
- Check your thesis again: does it take an actual position, or does it
  sit on the fence ("there are advantages and disadvantages to both")?
  A fence-sitting thesis is the single most common reason strong writers
  plateau at Band 7.

Log the band estimate, the time overage (if any), and whether you
produced a real counter-argument in `PROGRESS_LOG.md`.
