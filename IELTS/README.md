# IELTS (Academic)

## Format cheat sheet

| Section | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | 30 min + 10 min transfer | 4 recordings, 40 questions |
| Reading | 60 min | 3 passages, 40 questions |
| Writing | 60 min | Task 1 (150 words, describe a graph/chart/process, 20 min) + Task 2 (250 words, essay, 40 min) |
| Speaking | 11–14 min | Part 1 (intro/familiar topics), Part 2 (cue card, 2 min), Part 3 (discussion) |

Band scores run 0–9 in 0.5 increments. Overall band is the rounded average
of the four section bands.

## Band 7 vs Band 8 Writing — what actually separates them

This is the gap most people get stuck in, and it's not about "better
vocabulary":

- **Band 7:** Clear position throughout, but some over/under-development
  of ideas; some errors in complex sentences; some inaccuracy in word choice.
- **Band 8:** Fully developed response with fully extended and well-
  supported ideas; wide range of structures used flexibly and accurately
  with only occasional minor errors; wide vocabulary used with flexibility
  to convey precise meaning.

The jump is **precision and flexibility under pressure**, not "sounding
smart." A Band 8 essay uses simpler words placed exactly right more often
than it uses rare words placed approximately right.
