# IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 — Drill Set

**Rules every time:** 20 minutes, hard stop. 150 words minimum. No opinion,
no speculation about *why* the data looks this way unless the prompt is a
process/map (data-description tasks are reported, not argued). Write by
hand or in a plain text file with no spellcheck/grammar-check running.

## Band 7 vs Band 8 — what actually separates them here

- **Band 7:** A clear overview is present, but it may be tucked into the
  wrong place (often this is dropped by people who "ran out of time") or
  it restates a data point instead of identifying the real trend.
- **Band 8:** The overview is a standalone paragraph, written *before* the
  supporting detail, that names the 2–3 most significant features without
  listing every number. Numbers appear in the body paragraphs as evidence,
  not in the overview as the main content.

The single most common Band 6 tell: writing the report as a list of every
data point in order, with no overview at all. An examiner should be able
to read your overview paragraph alone and know what the chart shows.

## Self-grading rubric (score yourself 0–9 on each, be harsh)

1. **Task Achievement** — Is there a clear overview? Are key features
   selected and the right comparisons made (not just every number
   transcribed)? No opinion or speculation included?
2. **Coherence & Cohesion** — Overview first, then grouped body
   paragraphs (e.g., by category or by time period, not one sentence per
   data point); logical linking.
3. **Lexical Resource** — Range of trend/comparison language (beyond
   "increased" and "decreased" every time): *rose, climbed, surged,
   plateaued, fluctuated, outstripped, a marginal rise, a sharp decline*.
4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** — Correct tense for the data given
   (past for historical data, present for a generic process/map
   description), accurate comparatives/superlatives.

## Prompts (do one per session, rotate types)

### 1. Line graph — UK electricity generation by source (% of total)

| Year | Coal | Gas | Nuclear | Renewables |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2000 | 32% | 38% | 23% | 3% |
| 2005 | 33% | 35% | 20% | 4% |
| 2010 | 28% | 42% | 16% | 7% |
| 2015 | 23% | 30% | 21% | 25% |
| 2020 | 2% | 32% | 16% | 43% |

**Task:** The table shows the percentage of UK electricity generated from
four sources between 2000 and 2020. Summarize the information by
selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where
relevant.

### 2. Bar chart — Household internet access by country (%)

| Country | 2010 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| South Korea | 83% | 98% |
| Brazil | 27% | 81% |
| Nigeria | 11% | 55% |
| Germany | 82% | 96% |

**Task:** The bar chart shows the percentage of households with internet
access in four countries in 2010 and 2023. Summarize the information by
selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where
relevant.

### 3. Process diagram — Municipal water purification (describe in words)

Stages, in order: (1) Raw water is drawn from a reservoir into a holding
tank. (2) Coarse screens remove large debris. (3) Chemical coagulants are
added, causing fine particles to clump into "floc." (4) The water passes
through a sedimentation basin, where floc settles to the bottom and is
removed. (5) The remaining water is filtered through layers of sand and
gravel. (6) Chlorine is added for disinfection. (7) The treated water is
pumped into the municipal supply network.

**Task:** The diagram shows the process by which water is purified for
municipal use. Summarize the information by describing the main stages of
the process.

*(Process-diagram note: this is the one Task 1 type where you describe a
sequence rather than data — use sequencers: "first," "once X has
occurred," "subsequently," "at this stage," "finally" — and present
tense throughout, since it describes a repeatable process, not a
one-time historical event.)*

### 4. Maps — Town center, 1995 vs. present (describe in words)

**1995:** A single two-lane road runs through the town center, lined with
small independent shops. A park occupies the northeast corner. No
dedicated cycling infrastructure exists. A single primary school sits on
the west side.

**Present:** The two-lane road has been widened to four lanes with a
dedicated cycle path on both sides. The park has been reduced by a third
to accommodate a new shopping mall built on its southern edge. The
primary school has been demolished and replaced with a larger secondary
school. A new train station has been built on the previously undeveloped
east side of town.

**Task:** The maps show the town center in 1995 and today. Summarize the
information by describing the main changes, and make comparisons where
relevant.

## After you write: the honest 10-minute audit

- Cover the data table/description. Read only your overview paragraph.
  Could someone reconstruct the main story from it alone? If not, your
  overview is a data point, not an overview.
- Count how many individual numbers appear in your overview — more than
  one or two is a sign you're listing, not summarizing.
- Check verb tense consistency: historical data = past tense throughout;
  process/map-as-general-description = present tense throughout. Mixing
  tenses within one report is a common, easily fixed Band 6 error.
- Count linking/comparison words used more than twice without variation.
