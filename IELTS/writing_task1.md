# IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 — Drill Set

**Timer: 20 minutes per task, 150 words minimum. Set a real timer. Task 1
is 1/3 of your Writing score and the section people train least — that
imbalance is exactly why it's usually the band that's dragging your
average down.**

Task 1 is NOT an essay. No opinion, no "I think." You are a reporter: look
at the data, report what it shows, and stop. The #1 reason people cap at
Band 6 here is writing an essay's worth of generic sentences around a
chart they never actually describe with numbers.

## The structure that works every time

1. **Paraphrase the prompt** (1 sentence) — don't copy it verbatim.
2. **Overview** (1–2 sentences) — the 2–3 biggest overall trends/
   comparisons, with NO specific numbers. This is the single
   highest-value paragraph and the one most people skip. Examiners are
   explicitly told to check for it; skipping it caps you at Band 5 for
   Task Achievement regardless of how good the rest is.
3. **Body paragraph 1** — detail the most significant features, with
   specific data to support them.
4. **Body paragraph 2** — remaining significant features, with data.

Never describe every single data point. Select and report the
*significant* features — that word is in the official band descriptors.

## Band 7 vs Band 8 here

- **Band 7:** Clear overview present; key features covered but some may
  be missing or over-detailed; data used accurately; some errors in
  complex sentences.
- **Band 8:** Overview clearly presents all key trends; fully covers key
  features with no omissions or unnecessary inclusions; wide range of
  cohesive devices and comparative structures used precisely.

The jump is **selection discipline** — Band 8 reports exactly the
features that matter, not more, not fewer — and variety in how you
express comparison (not just "higher than" every time: also "nearly
double," "a steady decline," "fluctuated before recovering," etc.).

## Task 4 — Process diagram

The diagram below shows the process of producing bottled drinking water.

```
Raw water source (spring/well)
        ↓
Collection & transport to plant
        ↓

        ↓
UV sterilization (kills pathogens)
        ↓
Mineral adjustment (adds back minerals)
        ↓
Bottling & sealing (automated line)
        ↓
Labeling
        ↓
Packaging into crates
        ↓
Distribution to retailers
```

Note: process diagrams need a different overview — state how many stages
there are and where the process starts/ends, not a "trend."

**Write 150+ words. 20 minutes.**

## Task 5 — Map (two time periods)

The maps show the town of Fairview in 1990 and in 2020.

**1990:** small town center with a market square, one school, farmland
surrounding the whole town on all four sides, a single road running
north–south through the center.

**2020:** market square replaced by a shopping mall; two schools (original
one expanded, new one built in the north); farmland on the south and west
converted to a residential housing estate; farmland on the east
unchanged; a new ring road built around the town connecting to the
original north–south road; a small park added where the east farmland
meets the housing estate.

**Write 150+ words. 20 minutes.**

## Self-grading checklist (do this after every task, before moving on)

- [ ] Overview present, with no specific numbers in it, covering the 2–3
      biggest patterns?
- [ ] Did I select significant features only — not every data point, not
      zero data points?
- [ ] Did I use at least 3 different comparative/trend structures (not
      "X was higher than Y" five times in a row)?
- [ ] Word count 150+? (Count it — don't estimate.)
- [ ] Zero opinion words ("I think," "in my view," "surprisingly")?
      Task 1 is purely descriptive.
- [ ] At least one complex sentence per paragraph, and does it actually
      hold together grammatically when read aloud?

### Common phrases by data pattern (vary these — don't lock onto one)

- **Rising:** rose, climbed, increased, grew, surged (sharp), crept up
  (slow)
- **Falling:** fell, declined, dropped, plummeted (sharp), tapered off
  (slow)
- **Flat:** remained stable, plateaued, leveled off, held steady at
- **Fluctuating:** fluctuated, varied, oscillated
- **Comparing:** nearly double, roughly half, marginally higher,
  considerably lower, on par with, in contrast to

### Scoring

Score yourself against the checklist above (12 items): 11–12 = Band 8
pace · 8–10 = Band 7 pace · below 8 = Band 6 or lower — identify which
specific item you keep missing and log it in `PROGRESS_LOG.md`'s
recurring mistake tracker. The overview is the single highest-leverage
fix: if that box is unchecked more than once, fix nothing else until it
is.
