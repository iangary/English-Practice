# IELTS Writing Task 2 — Drill Set

**Rules every time:** 40 minutes, hard stop. 250 words minimum (no upper
limit, but padding past ~300 costs you time you need for accuracy). Write
by hand or in a plain text file with no spellcheck/grammar-check running —
the real exam gives you neither.

## Self-grading rubric (score yourself 0–9 on each, be harsh)

1. **Task Response** — Did you directly answer every part of the question?
   A partial answer caps you at Band 6 no matter how good the English is.
2. **Coherence & Cohesion** — Clear paragraphing (intro, 2–3 body, conclusion);
   logical progression; linking words used naturally, not mechanically
   ("Furthermore" in every paragraph is a Band 6 tell, not a Band 8 one).
3. **Lexical Resource** — Range + precision. Circle every word you weren't
   100% sure was correct. More than 2–3 circles in a 250-word essay = this
   is your bottleneck.
4. **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** — Mix of simple and complex sentences.
   Count your errors per 100 words. Over 3 errors/100 words is Band 6
   territory regardless of sentence complexity.

Your overall Task 2 band is roughly the average of the four, rounded down
if Task Response is weak (a brilliantly written answer to the wrong
question is still a weak answer).

## Prompts (do one per session, rotate types)

**Opinion (agree/disagree):**
1. Some people believe that unpaid community service should be a
   compulsory part of high school education. To what extent do you agree
   or disagree?
2. Many argue that university education should be free for all citizens,
   regardless of their financial background. Discuss both views and give
   your own opinion.
3. In many countries, the gap between the salaries of the highest and
   lowest paid employees is increasing. What problems does this cause, and
   what solutions can you suggest?

**Discuss both views:**
4. Some people think that children should begin learning a foreign
   language as soon as they start school, while others believe it is
   better to wait until secondary school. Discuss both views and give your
   opinion.
5. Some believe that the main purpose of science is to make life easier;
   others believe that science creates more problems than it solves.
   Discuss both views and give your opinion.

**Problem/solution:**
6. As cities grow larger, so does the volume of traffic. What problems
   does this cause, and what measures could be taken to reduce traffic
   congestion?
7. An increasing number of people are choosing to work from home rather
   than commute to an office. What are the causes of this trend, and what
   effects does it have on individuals and society?

**Two-part question:**
8. Many museums charge for admission while others are free. Do you think
   this is a positive or negative development?
9. Some people think governments should spend more money on the arts.
   Others believe this money should be spent in other ways. Which do you
   agree with?

**Advantages/disadvantages:**
10. Increasing numbers of people are choosing cosmetic surgery to change
    their appearance. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this
    trend?

## Set 2 — higher difficulty (abstract / layered questions)

These are deliberately more abstract than Set 1 — no concrete policy to
describe, which means you have to generate the examples yourself instead
of leaning on an obvious real-world case. That's exactly where Band 6–7
essays stall: generic claims with no specific example to anchor them.

**Opinion (agree/disagree):**
11. "Success in life is determined more by luck than by hard work." To
    what extent do you agree or disagree?
12. Some people believe that the ability to work well with others is more
    important for career success than individual talent. To what extent
    do you agree or disagree?

**Discuss both views:**
13. Some argue that true happiness comes from achieving personal goals,
    while others believe it comes from contributing to the well-being of
    others. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
14. Some people think that traditional customs and values should be
    preserved even as societies modernize; others think they inevitably
    become obsolete and should be allowed to fade. Discuss both views and
    give your opinion.

**Problem/solution:**
15. In many countries, young people are increasingly disengaged from
    local and national politics. What are the causes of this, and what
    measures could be taken to increase their engagement?
16. The constant availability of news and information has led some
    people to feel overwhelmed and anxious. What problems does this
    cause, and what can be done to address them?

**Two-part question:**
17. Some people believe that artificial intelligence will eventually
    replace the need for human judgment in most professional fields. Is
    this a positive or negative development?
18. Many believe that the purpose of a university education should be
    purely academic, while others think it should also prepare students
    for the job market directly. Which view do you agree with?

**Advantages/disadvantages:**
19. An increasing number of people are relying on social media as their
    primary source of news. What are the advantages and disadvantages of
    this trend?
20. More companies are allowing employees to choose their own working
    hours rather than fixed schedules. What are the advantages and
    disadvantages of this flexibility?

## After you write: the honest 10-minute audit

- Underline your thesis in the intro. Can you find it in under 5 seconds?
  If not, rewrite it to be one unambiguous sentence.
- Check each body paragraph has exactly one main idea, one explanation,
  and one specific example (not a vague generalization dressed as one).
- Count linking words. If you used the same one twice, replace the
  second instance.
- Read your conclusion alone, without the essay above it. Does it
  summarize your actual argument, or just restate the question?
